Mount Sanqing (三清山)

Description for Research Study

A UNESCO World Heritage site, Mount Sanqing is a renowned Taoist sacred mountain offering rich opportunities for research. Its unique granite peak forest landscape, with striking rock formations, caves, and waterfalls, is ideal for geological and geographical studies. As a sacred site, it's pivotal for studies in religion (Taoism), cultural history, and the sociology of pilgrimage. The mountain's diverse vertical climate zones also support a wide range of flora and fauna, making it suitable for biodiversity research and ecological studies. The area's aesthetic beauty and its depiction in art and literature could also be a focus for cultural and art history research.

Wuyuan County (婺源县)

Description for Research Study

Wuyuan County, often dubbed "China's most beautiful countryside," provides a vast canvas for research. It is particularly significant for studies of traditional Huizhou culture and architecture, as it preserves a large number of ancient villages with well-maintained Tang, Song, Ming, and Qing dynasty buildings. Research themes include vernacular architecture, village planning, cultural landscape evolution, and the preservation of intangible cultural heritage (e.g., local crafts, dialects like the Hui dialect, and customs). Its famed rapeseed flower fields in terraced landscapes (like in Jiangling) offer scope for agro-ecological studies, rural tourism impact analysis, and landscape aesthetics research. The county's historical shift from Anhui to Jiangxi could also be a point for socio-political studies.

Poyang Lake - Shangrao Section (Poyang County) (鄱阳湖 - 上饶部分 (鄱阳县))

Description for Research Study

As China's largest freshwater lake and a Ramsar Wetland of International Importance, the Shangrao-accessible parts of Poyang Lake are critical for ecological and environmental research. It's a vital habitat for migratory birds, especially the endangered Siberian Crane (90% of the global population winters here), making it a prime location for ornithological studies, bird migration tracking, and wetland conservation research. Studies can also focus on the impact of human activities (sand dredging, fishing moratoriums, surrounding agriculture, and upstream projects like the Three Gorges Dam) on the lake's hydrology, water quality, and biodiversity, including the critically endangered Finless Porpoise. Limnology, freshwater ecology, and the socio-economic impacts of environmental changes on local communities are also key research areas.

Key Research Themes

  • Wetland Ecology
  • Ornithology (migratory birds, Siberian Cranes)
  • Freshwater Ecology
  • Conservation (Finless Porpoise)
  • Environmental Science
  • Hydrology
  • Limnology
  • Human-Environment Interaction
  • Socio-economic Impacts of Environmental Change
